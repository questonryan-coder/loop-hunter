# Loop Hunter 🌙

A free listening game from **AuraFlow** — original soundscapes for sleep, focus & calm.

**Play:** https://questonryan-coder.github.io/loop-hunter/

A stream of short soundscape segments plays one after another, each cut from a real
published AuraFlow session (Midnight Forest, Haunted Rain, Gothic Cathedral,
Fog Over the Moors, The Cellar, Witching Hour, Pumpkin Hollow, Candlelit Seance, Lantern Keeper, Graveyard Shift, Autumn Rain, Crackling Fireplace — 56 segments).
Somewhere in the stream, one earlier segment secretly repeats. Tap **THAT'S A LOOP!**
the moment you hear the repeat. Catch it to build your streak — cry wolf or let it
slip past, and the run ends.

Hosted by **KONG** 🦍 — the warrior-king on his stone throne keeps this stream,
and thanks you personally on the result screen.

Four ways to play: **Free play** (endless rounds, one mistake ends the run),
the **Daily Challenge** (one seeded stream per Phoenix day), **Timed Hunt**
(90 seconds on the clock — catch as many loops as you can; a false call costs
5 seconds, and only the clock ends the run), and **Endless Drift** (no clock,
no game over — longer, gentler rounds; catches and streaks pile up for as
long as you care to drift). Catch streaks are celebrated in every mode, and
if the hunt made your night, there's a quiet tip jar for the maker on the
home and result screens.

Full hour-long sessions: https://auraflowfocus.com
